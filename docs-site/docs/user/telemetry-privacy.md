# Telemetry & privacy

The first time a scan finishes, ROZOOM shows a banner asking whether you want to share anonymous usage data — it only asks after you've actually seen the app do something, not before.

Nothing is sent before you answer, not even the fact that you added a cluster. Choose **No thanks** and nothing leaves your machine.

You can change this later in **Fleet Settings**.
